How it works

How our salon customer
acquisition system works

We bring potential customers to your salon. You turn them into customers. The process is simple and transparent.

01

We understand your salon

Before a single rupee of ad spend, we learn what makes your salon work.

  • Location
  • Services
  • Pricing
  • Offers
  • Target customers
  • Capacity
  • Competition
02

We create your campaign

We select the services and offers worth promoting, then build the advertising strategy and campaign creatives around them.

03

We run Google & Meta ads

We launch targeted advertising campaigns across the three channels that actually drive salon footfall.

  • Google
  • Instagram
  • Facebook
04

Customers contact you

Interested customers reach your salon directly — no middle layer, no delay.

  • WhatsApp
  • Phone
  • Booking form
05

You convert the customer

Your team handles the enquiry, the appointment and the salon service — the part you're already great at.

06

Customer makes their first purchase

When the customer visits your salon and pays for a service, they're recorded as a campaign-generated customer.

07

We receive 10%

We receive 10% of that first purchase value. That's our entire fee.

08

No more charges

After the first purchase there is no further commission from that customer. Future purchases belong completely to your salon.
